Project Title:
Tilak Nagar Civic Pulse: Resident Satisfaction Analytics for Local Government Services

Survey Purpose:
This survey collects residents' opinions about basic local government services in Tilak Nagar, Mumbai.

Data Collection Method:
Responses are collected through Google Forms.

Rating Scale:
1 = Very dissatisfied
2 = Dissatisfied
3 = Neutral
4 = Satisfied
5 = Very satisfied

Survey Questions:
1. Do you agree to participate in this survey?
2. Which locality do you live in?
3. What is your age group?
4. How long have you lived in this area?
5. How satisfied are you with the regularity of water supply?
6. How satisfied are you with garbage collection?
7. How satisfied are you with the cleanliness of public areas?
8. How satisfied are you with the condition of roads and footpaths?
9. How satisfied are you with street lighting in your area?
10. How satisfied are you with drainage and sewage services?
11. How satisfied are you with the availability and cleanliness of public toilets?
12. How satisfied are you with online municipal services or complaint systems?
13. Have you ever reported a local civic problem?
14. Overall, how satisfied are you with local government services in your area?
15. Which local service needs the most urgent improvement, and what is your suggestion?

Privacy:
The survey should not collect names, phone numbers, exact addresses, passwords or other sensitive personal information.

Current Status:
Survey data collection is currently in progress.
